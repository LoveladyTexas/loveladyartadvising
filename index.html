The Firm

Lovelady Art Advising is a Houston-based fine art consulting and appraisal firm working with individual collectors, galleries, and institutions.

The firm pairs appraisal training with hands-on experience in installation and handling — so acquisition, valuation, and care are considered together, not as separate steps handed off between different people.
